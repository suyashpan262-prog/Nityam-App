## ✨ Key Features

* **Daily Habit Tracker (Today View):** Track your daily routines, build consistency, and maintain your daily streaks effortlessly.

* **Sacred Verses:** Read daily inspiring Sanskrit shlokas and spiritual verses to stay motivated and grounded.
* **Smart Notes:** Capture your daily thoughts, reflections, and important tasks with the built-in note editor.
* **Bilingual Support (English & Hindi):** Switch seamlessly between English and Hindi (हिंदी) anytime within the app.
